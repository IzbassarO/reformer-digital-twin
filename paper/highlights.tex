\section*{Highlights}
\begin{itemize}
\item Coupled tube, furnace and creep model of an SMR calibrated on plant data with CV
\item Firing, steam-to-carbon and excess air explain 82\% of hot-spot temperature variance
\item Load-following costs under 2\% life per kmol H$_2$; catalyst ageing costs 2.3 times more
\item A Pareto knee gives 16\% more hydrogen at 18\% less fuel and 28\% of base life cost
\item Creep-curve scatter dominates life uncertainty; flame length vs load is the key gap
\end{itemize}
