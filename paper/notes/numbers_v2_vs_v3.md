## v3 UQ run: variance shares, log10 t_r interval and robustness fractions, per curve

The base-alloy column is read straight from `data/uq/uq_summary_v3.json`. The other three curves are **cheap** to add
without re-running the Monte Carlo: `T_wo_max_K`, `sigma_hot_MPa` and `H2_net_kmol_h` are bit-identical across alloys, and
the two creep parameters enter analytically as `log10 t_r = LMP(sigma)/(scale*T) - C + scatter`. Each of the 2048 v3 draws
is re-used by its quantile, so every alloy sees the same random numbers and the comparison is paired sample for sample.
Reproducing the base alloy this way returns the published values to within 1e-4 (a grid-interpolation residual on the
polynomial inversion); the robustness fractions and variance shares reproduce exactly.
