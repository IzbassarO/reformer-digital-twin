Thermal-fatigue screening of the RQ2 scenarios  (v3 results)
============================================================================================================
SCREENING ONLY. No life, no cycles to failure and no creep-fatigue damage sum is computed or
implied below. The question is one of magnitude: how does the cyclic thermal-stress exposure of
smooth load variation compare with the exposure the plant already accepts at every cold start?

WHAT IS COMPUTED FROM THE TWIN, AND WHAT IS ASSUMED
------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
  computed by the twin :
    - the hot-spot wall drop dT_wall = q_o d_o/(2 lambda) ln(d_o/d_i) at every scenario hour,
      from the same coupled solve the scenario stage used;
    - the wall drop at each stage of the staged warm-up;
    - the rainflow cycle counts, which follow from those histories alone.
  assumed :
    - E = 150 GPa, alpha = 18e-6 /K, nu = 0.3: the single
      representative values of Section S1, not temperature-dependent properties;
    - 2 to 6 full cool-downs per year -- PLANT PRACTICE, not a computed result;
    - the Basquin exponents m = 3, 5, 8 are a sweep, not a fitted material property;
    - the elastic stress range is an upper bound: in service the thermal stress relaxes by creep.

CODE PATH FOR THE WALL DROP
------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
  dT_wall is not stored in the hourly histories, so it is recomputed here. A surrogate was tried
  and REJECTED: a GP fitted to the wall drop over the whole sampled operating box reaches only
  3.7 K RMSE (38 K worst case), the same order as the ranges being counted, and its noise would
  invent reversals in smooth histories. Every hour below is therefore the PHYSICS model, solved
  once per distinct operating point and broadcast over the hours that repeat it.

  LIMITATION -- scenarios the wall-drop calculation cannot represent:
    S4c_hot_band                   stored T_wo exceeds the physics solve by up to 40.0 K
    In these the scenario stage adds a metal-temperature offset directly to T_wo rather than
    changing an operating input, so re-solving the inputs returns the base state. The twin
    defines no wall gradient for an imposed offset -- an offset produced by extra local flux
    and one produced by a redistribution of radiation imply different gradients, and the
    scenario does not say which it is. The excursion is therefore ABSENT from the cycle counts
    for those rows, which are consequently a lower bound. No flux is invented to fill the gap.

------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
  Cold end is exact (a tube at ambient with no firing carries no flux, so the gradient is zero).

  stress range of ONE full start-up/shutdown cycle : 109.79 MPa at the hot spot (wall drop 56.93 K),
                                                    122.37 MPa on the maximum gradient (63.45 K)
  The intermediate stages do not enter that number. Cold -> hot -> cold is monotone in each
  direction, so it rainflow-counts as one full cycle of the end-to-end range whatever path the
  warm-up takes; the stages are shown to make the gradient's growth visible, not to set the
  range. A start-up interrupted by holds or a trip would add sub-cycles, which are not modelled.
  at the assumed 2-6 cool-downs per year that is 2-6 such cycles annually

  NOTE: Section S1 of the supplementary material quotes a 20-30 K wall drop and about 45 MPa.
  The twin gives 56.9 K and 110 MPa at the hot spot of the
  calibrated base. The relation and the constants used here are the ones printed there; only the
  wall drop is taken from the model. The discrepancy is reported, not resolved.

WHERE THE DAILY CYCLE BECOMES COMPARABLE TO A START-UP
------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
    m = 3: one year of S2_daily = 7.77 start-up cycles  -- above the whole assumed annual start-up exposure
    m = 5: one year of S2_daily = 0.597 start-up cycles  -- below one start-up
    m = 8: one year of S2_daily = 0.0127 start-up cycles  -- below one start-up

  Every scenario against the assumed annual start-up exposure (2-6 cool-downs per year):
    m = 3: above the band S2_daily   |   inside the band S3_renewable, S4b_severe_overfire
    m = 5: above the band none   |   inside the band none
    m = 8: above the band none   |   inside the band none

    crossing points for S2_daily (exponent above which a year is worth less than the target):
                                               hot spot   max gradient
      equal to ONE start-up                        4.60           4.15
      equal to 2 start-ups per year (low)          4.10           3.65
      equal to 6 start-ups per year (high)         3.25           2.90

    The answer therefore depends on the exponent, which is why it is swept rather than assumed.
    No fatigue curve for this alloy is used anywhere above, and none of these numbers is a life.
