Flame-length structural variant resolved into a threshold
==============================================================================================================
The robustness table reports each conclusion at the calibrated flame length and at a bounding
variant L_q_eff = L_q * load^0.5. Here the exponent p is swept instead, and the paired Monte
Carlo robustness fraction of each statement is recomputed at every p on the base alloy curve.

  p = 0    is the calibrated, load-independent flame length  (nominal column of the table)
  p = 0.5  is the published bounding variant                 (variant column of the table)
  p is a structural assumption about the furnace, not a fitted quantity. The sweep says which
  value of it would change a conclusion; it does not claim a value.

WHAT WAS RE-RUN
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
  load^p = 1 for any p when the load is 1, so a full-load point cannot move with the exponent.
  re-run at every p : RQ3_knee, load_0.70, load_0.85  (loads 1.100, 0.700, 0.850)
  read from the stored campaign unchanged, all at load 1.000 : S1_base, S5_y4_holdslip_end,
  S6_SC2.5, S6_SC3.5, load_1.00
  Two of the five fractions therefore cannot move with p, and are shown flat as a check rather
  than as a result.
